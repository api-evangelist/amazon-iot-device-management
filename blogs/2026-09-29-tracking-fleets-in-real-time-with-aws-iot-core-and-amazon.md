Connecting dedicated GPS hardware to AWS IoT Core and visualizing positions with Amazon Location Service gives fleet operators a live view of every vehicle. This post covers the concepts, architecture, and cost trade-offs of a lightweight, serverless approach to GPS-focused fleet tracking for small to medium-sized fleets.
